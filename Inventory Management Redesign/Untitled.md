## Interviewee Screener Criteria

- Must be a GM, ops manager or experienced shift lead
  - Someone who routinely deals with ordering and knows when items will be in stock and not
  - Aware of limited time offer items
- Restaurant should be one where preordering is common
  - Restaurants with only ordering for ASAP don't really have this problem
- Toast preferred, but other POS is acceptable
  - Get a broader picture of the state of tools for this problem
