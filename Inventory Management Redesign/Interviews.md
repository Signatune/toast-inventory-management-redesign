# Interview Script

## Opening / Consent
*(~2 min — read or paraphrase)*

"Hi [name], thanks so much for making time. I'm Mike — I'm a UX designer working on improving inventory management tools for restaurant operators.

Quick housekeeping:
- This will take about 25–30 minutes
- I'd love to record the audio so I can focus on listening, not typing — is that okay? It's for my notes only and won't be shared externally
- I may quote you (anonymized) in research documents or design presentations
- You can skip any question or stop at any time

I want to be clear: I'm not testing you. There are no right or wrong answers. I'm trying to understand how your operation actually works so I can design something useful, not something I made up in a vacuum."

# Warmup Questions
*(5–8 min)*

★ 1. Tell me about your restaurant
★ 2. What's your role day-to-day? Do you deal with inventory often?
★ 3. Walk me through a typical prep day

> [!tip] Probe
> Note when in the day inventory decisions happen. If they mention a specific tool or list, ask: "Can you show me what that looks like?"

&nbsp;&nbsp;4. What POS do you use currently? Did you ever use any other systems?
★ 5. How do you track stock levels currently?
&nbsp;&nbsp;6. Who on your team deals with stock levels?

---

# Preorders & Future-Date Stock Management
*(10–15 min)*

## Preorder Workflow
*(5–7 min)*

★ 1. Do you accept preorders for future dates? Walk me through what happens from when the order comes in to when it's fulfilled.
★ 2. When a preorder comes in for, say, next Saturday — how do you know if you can fulfill it? What do you check?
3. Have you ever had to cancel or modify a preorder because you ran out of something? Tell me about that.

> [!tip] Probe
> Push for specifics: "Roughly how many orders? What was the item? What did you do?" Concrete numbers make the best quotes.

4. How do you currently decide how much stock to allocate for today vs. future orders? Is there a system or is it gut feel?

## Oversell/Undersell Tension
*(3–5 min)*

★ 5. Tell me about a time you had to choose between limiting stock to protect today's sales vs. keeping it open for preorders. What happened?
★ 6. When you set a stock quantity in Toast [or their POS], what does that number actually represent to you — today's count, this week's count, or something else?
&nbsp;&nbsp;7. If you could set a different stock quantity for each day of the week, how would that change your workflow?

## Workarounds
*(2–3 min)*

8. What's your current workaround for managing preorder inventory? Spreadsheets, mental notes, separate menu items, something else?
9. How much time per day/week do you spend on these workarounds?

> [!tip] Probe
> If they say "not that much," ask: "What about across the whole week — is this something you're thinking about every day or just when a big order comes in?"

---

# 86-ing & Mid-Service Inventory Crises
*(5–8 min)*

## 86-ing Workflow
*(3–5 min)*

★ 1. Tell me about the last time you had to 86 something mid-service. What happened, and how did you handle it?

> [!tip] Probe
> Ask for the emotional context: "What was that like for you and your team? Did customers find out?"

2. When you 86 an item, how many places do you have to update? POS, online ordering, catering menu, third-party apps?
★ 3. Do you have items that share ingredients — like multiple sandwiches that all use turkey? What happens when that ingredient runs out?
3. How do you communicate an 86 to your front-of-house team? How quickly does it actually propagate?

## Cross-Channel Conflicts
*(2–3 min)*

★ 5. Do you sell the same items across multiple channels — dine-in, online, catering, delivery apps? How do you manage inventory across them?
★ 6. Have you ever had a situation where something sold out on one channel but was still showing available on another? What happened?
7. If all your channels pulled from one shared inventory pool, what would that solve for you? What problems might it create?

---

# Future Planning & Feature Wishlist
*(7–12 min)*

## Future Planning
*(3–5 min)*

★ 1. How do you plan stock for the upcoming week? Is it the same every week or does it change?
&nbsp;&nbsp;2. What information do you wish you had when planning how much to make or order for a specific day?
&nbsp;&nbsp;3. Do you have days that are predictably different — weekends, holidays, event days? How do you adjust inventory for those?
&nbsp;&nbsp;4. If you could look at a calendar view of your inventory for the next two weeks, what would you want to see on it?

## Wishlist & Concept Reaction
*(3–5 min)*

★ 5. If you could change one thing about how your POS handles inventory, what would it be?
★ 6. Imagine you could set a specific stock quantity for each item on each future date — like saying "we'll have 30 turkey sandwiches available Saturday." How useful would that be on a scale of 1–10? Why?

> [!tip] Probe
> If they say 7 or higher: "What would make it a 10?" If lower: "What's missing for it to be more useful?"

&nbsp;&nbsp;7. Who on your team would use a feature like that? Would it be just you, or would you want shift leads to have access too?
★ 8. What would make you not use a feature like that — what would make it too complicated or not worth the effort?

## Closing
*(1–2 min)*

★ 9. Is there anything about managing inventory that I haven't asked about but you think is important?
★ 10. Would you be open to looking at a prototype in a few weeks and giving feedback?
