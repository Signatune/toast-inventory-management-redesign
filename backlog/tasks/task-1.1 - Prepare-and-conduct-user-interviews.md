## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Conduct 3-5 interviews with restaurant operators (20-30 min each) to validate pain points and gather direct quotes for the case study. This is the highest-priority step and cannot be delegated to AI.

Discussion guide topics:
- Current workarounds for preorder stock management
- Last time they lost a sale due to inventory conflict
- Last time they had to 86 something mid-service
- How they currently plan stock for future days

Goal: Capture direct quotes that anchor the problem statement in real operator experience. These quotes will be used throughout the case study to ground design decisions in user reality.
