## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create the closing section of the discussion guide. These questions look forward — how operators currently plan for future days, what they wish existed, and a lightweight concept validation of the scheduling feature.

Specific questions to ask:

**Future planning (3-5 min)**
- "How do you plan stock for the upcoming week? Is it the same every week or does it change?"
- "What information do you wish you had when planning how much to make or order for a specific day?"
- "Do you have days that are predictably different — weekends, holidays, event days? How do you adjust inventory for those?"
- "If you could look at a calendar view of your inventory for the next two weeks, what would you want to see on it?"

**Wishlist and concept reaction (3-5 min)**
- "If you could change one thing about how your POS handles inventory, what would it be?"
- "Imagine you could set a specific stock quantity for each item on each future date — like saying 'we'll have 30 turkey sandwiches available Saturday.' How useful would that be on a scale of 1-10? Why?"
- "Who on your team would use a feature like that? Would it be just you, or would you want shift leads to have access too?"
- "What would make you not use a feature like that — what would make it too complicated or not worth the effort?"

**Closing (1-2 min)**
- "Is there anything about managing inventory that I haven't asked about but you think is important?"
- "Would you be open to looking at a prototype in a few weeks and giving feedback?"

Goal: Validate that the proposed feature direction resonates before you invest in design. The 1-10 scale question gives you a quotable data point. The "what would make you NOT use it" question surfaces adoption barriers early.
