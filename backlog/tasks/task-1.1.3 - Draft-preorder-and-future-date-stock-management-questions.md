## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create the core discussion guide section covering Pain Point 1 — no date-based stock quantities. This is the primary pain point and the feature you're designing for, so it needs the deepest coverage.

Specific questions to ask:

**Preorder workflow (5-7 min)**
- "Do you accept preorders for future dates? Walk me through what happens from when the order comes in to when it's fulfilled."
- "When a preorder comes in for, say, next Saturday — how do you know if you can fulfill it? What do you check?"
- "Have you ever had to cancel or modify a preorder because you ran out of something? Tell me about that."
- "How do you currently decide how much stock to allocate for today vs. future orders? Is there a system or is it gut feel?"

**The oversell/undersell tension (3-5 min)**
- "Tell me about a time you had to choose between limiting stock to protect today's sales vs. keeping it open for preorders. What happened?"
- "When you set a stock quantity in Toast [or their POS], what does that number actually represent to you — today's count, this week's count, or something else?"
- "If you could set a different stock quantity for each day of the week, how would that change your workflow?"

**Workarounds (2-3 min)**
- "What's your current workaround for managing preorder inventory? Spreadsheets, mental notes, separate menu items, something else?"
- "How much time per day/week do you spend on these workarounds?"

Goal: Get the participant to tell specific stories with real numbers. "We had 40 babka preorders for Rosh Hashanah but..." is infinitely more powerful than "it's hard to manage preorders."
