## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Combine all question sections into a single, print-ready discussion guide document. Add interview logistics, consent framing, and note-taking protocol.

Should include:
- **Intro script**: Who you are, why you're doing this, how long it will take, consent for recording/quoting
- **Question flow**: All sections in order with time estimates (total should hit 20-30 min)
- **Probe reminders**: Marginal notes like "dig deeper if they mention a specific incident" or "ask for numbers"
- **Note-taking template**: Columns for timestamp, quote, topic tag, and emotional tone
- **Debrief checklist**: 3-5 things to write down immediately after each interview (top quote, biggest surprise, validation/invalidation of assumptions)

The guide should be conversational, not robotic — questions are starting points, not a rigid script. Mark which questions are must-ask vs. nice-to-have so you can flex timing based on where the conversation goes.
