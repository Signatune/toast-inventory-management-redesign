## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Walk 1-2 restaurant operators through the interactive prototype and document findings. This is the usability validation step before final write-up.

Session structure:
- Brief context setting (don't over-explain the feature)
- Task-based walkthrough: ask the operator to complete specific scenarios using the prototype
- Observe where they hesitate, get confused, or express delight
- Capture direct quotes about what works and what doesn't
- Note any workflow gaps or assumptions that were wrong

Suggested test scenarios:
- "You just received a delivery of turkey. Set it up so your turkey sandwiches are available starting tomorrow with a count of 40."
- "You're selling a special holiday item for one week only, limit 50. Set that up."
- "It's mid-service and you just ran out of the soup. Handle that."

Document findings in a format that supports the case study write-up: what changed, what was validated, what would change in a next iteration.
