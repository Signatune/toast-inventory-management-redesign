## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create the opening section of the discussion guide. These questions establish rapport, understand the participant's operation, and set the stage for the pain point deep-dives.

Specific questions to ask:

**Intro (2-3 min)**
- "Tell me about your restaurant — what type of food, how many locations, how long you've been operating."
- "What's your role day-to-day? How much of your time goes to inventory vs. other ops?"
- "Walk me through a typical prep day — how do you decide what to make and how much?"

**Current tools (2-3 min)**
- "What POS/inventory system are you on? How long have you been using it?"
- "How do you currently track stock levels — in the system, on paper, in your head, some combination?"
- "Who on your team touches inventory settings? Is it just you or do others have access?"

These questions warm the participant up, give you operational context to reference later, and surface how centralized or ad-hoc their inventory management is.
