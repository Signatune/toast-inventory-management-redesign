## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Review the completed interview discussion guide in the Obsidian vault at `Inventory Management Redesign/Interviews.md`.

The guide covers the full arc of a 25–30 min interview:
- Opening/consent script
- Warmup (5–8 min)
- Preorders & Future-Date Stock Management (10–15 min)
- 86-ing & Mid-Service Inventory Crises (5–8 min)
- Future Planning & Feature Wishlist (7–12 min)
- Note-taking template
- Post-interview debrief checklist

Questions are marked ★ (must-ask) vs. unmarked (nice-to-have), and probe reminders are embedded as Obsidian callouts.

**Review checklist:**
- Does the opening script feel natural to say out loud? Adjust wording as needed.
- Are the must-ask/nice-to-have designations correct for your priorities?
- Are the probe reminders placed on the right questions?
- Does the question flow feel like a conversation, or does it jump around?
- Is anything missing from the note-taking template or debrief checklist?
- Does the total timing feel realistic based on your experience with operators?
