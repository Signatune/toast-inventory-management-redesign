## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Create the discussion guide section covering Pain Points 2 and 3 — shared ingredient limitations and cross-channel inventory conflicts. These surface the operational chaos that happens when the system fails.

Specific questions to ask:

**86-ing workflow (3-5 min)**
- "Tell me about the last time you had to 86 something mid-service. What happened, and how did you handle it?"
- "When you 86 an item, how many places do you have to update? POS, online ordering, catering menu, third-party apps?"
- "Do you have items that share ingredients — like multiple sandwiches that all use turkey? What happens when that ingredient runs out?"
- "How do you communicate an 86 to your front-of-house team? How quickly does it actually propagate?"

**Cross-channel conflicts (2-3 min)**
- "Do you sell the same items across multiple channels — dine-in, online, catering, delivery apps? How do you manage inventory across them?"
- "Have you ever had a situation where something sold out on one channel but was still showing available on another? What happened?"
- "If all your channels pulled from one shared inventory pool, what would that solve for you? What problems might it create?"

Goal: Capture the emotional weight of mid-service inventory failures — the stress, the customer impact, the staff friction. These quotes humanize the problem beyond a feature request.
